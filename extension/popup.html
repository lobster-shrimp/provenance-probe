provenance-probe

Point this at YOUR hosted instance, then capture one AI chat request from your own browser.

How to capture

  1. Open your AI chat app in a tab and log in.
  2. Open DevTools (F12) and select the Provenance Capture panel.
  3. Click Arm capture, send one short message, then pick the request and confirm the cookie prompt.

Credentials are stored only in this browser (chrome.storage) and are sent only to the instance URL above, over HTTPS. Nothing is captured unless you explicitly arm it in the DevTools panel.
